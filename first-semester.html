My first portfolio

About the project

Bringing a semester of work together

The portfolio was the final project of my first semester. Its purpose was to collect my assignments in one coherent digital experience and show how my skills had developed across web design, UX, content and frontend development.

Each project received its own case page, where I presented the idea, process and final result. The website therefore became both a personal presentation and a record of my progress as a new designer.

Visual direction

A personal retro-inspired identity

I wanted the portfolio to feel personal rather than look like a traditional school website. I developed a warm visual identity with brown, orange and yellow tones, playful typography and graphic details inspired by retro design.

The same visual language was used across the navigation, project cards, buttons and case pages to make the website feel consistent.

Looking back

The starting point for my current portfolio

This project was my first attempt at combining personal branding, case presentation and frontend development in one website. It taught me how important hierarchy, consistency and navigation are when presenting creative work.

I later used the experience from this project as the foundation for redesigning my current portfolio with a stronger focus on visual storytelling, video production and the kind of creative work I want to pursue professionally.
