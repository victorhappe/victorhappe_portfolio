The Photoplay

A Little Luck Can Change Everything

✦

A New Suit is a short silent film that tells the story of a man who dreams of a better life. The film follows a homeless man who dreams of living a completely different life. In his dream, he is wealthy, well-dressed and enjoying the good life — until he suddenly wakes up on a bench and is brought back to reality.

We follow him as he wanders through the streets, appearing lonely and down on his luck. Then, in the middle of nowhere, he discovers a suit hanging from a tree — remarkably similar to the one he was wearing in his dream.

He puts on the suit, the music changes and so does his mood. With a new sense of confidence and optimism, he continues his journey. The suit does not magically give him the life he dreamed of, but it brings him one small step closer to it.

II

Behind the Picture

Recreating the Silent Era

— ✦ —

The real world was designed to resemble an early silent film. Black-and-white imagery, film grain and a 4:3 frame help create the visual language of cinema from the 1920s.

Static compositions, exaggerated physical acting and simple visual comedy allow the story to be understood without spoken dialogue.

Music and carefully placed sound effects create rhythm, atmosphere and emotion throughout the film.

Two Worlds

One created on location. One created digitally.

The film opens inside a fantasy world — a deliberate contrast to the monochrome reality that follows.

The environment was created in Unreal Engine and used with an LED wall during production, combining a live-action performance with a digital world.

Final Notes

Storytelling Without Dialogue

✦

A New Suit challenged us to tell a complete story primarily through images, movement, editing and sound. Without dialogue, expressions, compositions and the timing of each cut became essential parts of the storytelling.

The project also gave me practical experience with virtual production and with combining Unreal Engine, an LED wall and traditional filmmaking within the same production.
