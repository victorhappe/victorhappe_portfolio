Høst Møn Festival

A digital redesign created to give Høst Møn a clearer identity, stronger atmosphere and a more intuitive festival experience.

Web designUX researchVisual identityContent production
Festival redesign

About the project

A stronger digital identity for the festival

Høst Møn is a Danish music festival with a relaxed, local and community-driven atmosphere. Our task was to redesign the festival's website and create a more cohesive digital expression.

The final concept focused on improving navigation, making the programme easier to explore and communicating the festival's warm, informal character more clearly.

Visual direction

Warm, simple and festival-focused

The visual identity was built around warm, sandy colours, bold typography and simple graphic elements inspired by nature, music and summer evenings.

Barlow Condensed was used for headings to create energy and character, while Inter supported readability across practical information and longer text.

The design system helped keep buttons, cards, spacing, typography and colours consistent across the full website.

Key feature

An interactive festival programme

The programme page was one of the most important parts of the redesign. Visitors needed to understand the lineup quickly without leaving the page or opening multiple separate artist profiles.

We created an interactive solution where hovering over an artist revealed an image and a short introduction. This made the programme more visual, engaging and easier to explore.

My contribution

My role in the project

  • Contributed to user research, interviews and usability testing
  • Helped develop the visual direction and design system
  • Designed layouts and interface elements in Figma
  • Worked on the structure and presentation of the festival programme
  • Contributed to content planning and visual storytelling
  • Helped translate research findings into concrete design decisions

What I learned

Designing for atmosphere and usability

This project strengthened my understanding of how research, visual identity and interface design work together. I learned how to create a website that is not only functional, but also communicates the feeling and personality of an event.

It also gave me experience in working with a shared design system and using user feedback to improve hierarchy, navigation and interaction.
