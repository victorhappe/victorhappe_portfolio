The creator behind the work

Curious mind.
Visual instincts.

Hi, I'm Victor Happe, a Multimedia Design student at EK – Business Academy Copenhagen. I am especially interested in video production, visual storytelling and digital content creation. Through my studies, I have worked with branding, UX, web design, frontend development, content production and video editing. I enjoy combining visuals, storytelling and digital experiences to communicate ideas in a clear and engaging way.

This autumn, I will specialize in Advanced Media Production, where I will further develop my skills in video production, post-production, animation, immersive media and transmedia storytelling. Alongside my studies, I have several years of experience from the service industry, where I have developed strong communication skills, responsibility and the ability to work well in busy team environments.

  • Design: Figma, Illustrator and visual identity
  • Web: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and responsive design
