Life Inside a Fridge

The idea

A bottle afraid of being next.

The story is told from the perspective of a bottle living inside a refrigerator with the other bottles around it. The bottle is humanised through emotions such as fear and loneliness.

Every time the refrigerator opens, a hand reaches inside and removes one of its “friends”. One by one they disappear until the bottle is left completely alone, not knowing what happens outside the fridge.

In the final moment, the screen turns black and the sound of the refrigerator door opening can be heard once again. The film ends before we discover what happens next, leaving the viewer with the same uncertainty as the bottle — not knowing whether the hand has returned for it or for something else.

Creating the horror feeling

Sound, perspective and suspense

The film uses a low first-person perspective to make the audience experience the refrigerator from the bottle's point of view. Close-ups and the large hand entering the frame make ordinary movements feel threatening.

Sound was especially important for creating the horror atmosphere. Voices and music from outside the fridge are intentionally muffled, while the refrigerator hum and a growing heartbeat make the bottle's fear feel more intense.

By combining these sounds with darkness, sudden light and slow pacing, we tried to make a completely normal situation feel suspenseful to the audience.

My contribution

My role in the film

  • Helped develop the concept and story
  • Planned framing and camera placement inside the fridge
  • Worked with the visual setup and shot sequence
  • Edited the film with focus on suspense, pacing and atmosphere
  • Used sound effects and timing to create the horror feeling

What I learned

Turning something ordinary into a story.

The project taught me how perspective, editing and sound can completely change how an everyday situation is experienced. It was also my first real introduction to using pacing and sound design as active storytelling tools rather than simply supporting the images.
